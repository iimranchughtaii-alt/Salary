Privacy Policy — Payroll

Offline Calculator · Local Storage Only

We do not collect any user data, all salary data is stored locally on the user's device.

1. Not a Loan or Lending App

This is not a loan app. Payroll does not offer, originate, broker, or facilitate loans, credit, or financial lending of any kind. It is strictly an offline salary, overtime, attendance, personal deduction tracker, and payslip calculation utility.

2. Data Collection & Storage

Payroll works completely offline without requiring user registration or login. Attendance logs, salary settings, deduction records, and generated payslips remain strictly inside local storage on your device. No personal or financial information is transmitted to or stored on external servers.

3. Permissions

Storage / file access is used solely when you choose to save or share a generated payslip (JPG/PDF) or JSON backup on your own device.
